## Abstract

This study examines intangible cultural heritage as both an object of protection and a source of cultural vitality within rural revitalization. It analyses challenges facing living heritage under modernization, including weakened value recognition, shrinking cultural spaces, and discontinuities in intergenerational transmission.

Through the Mazhuang sachet case in Xuzhou, the article develops practice-oriented pathways that connect local knowledge, inheritors, community participation, cultural communication, and sustainable development. The study shows how heritage protection can be embedded in contemporary rural life while retaining cultural specificity.

**Keywords:** intangible cultural heritage; rural revitalization; Mazhuang sachets; cultural transmission; community participation
