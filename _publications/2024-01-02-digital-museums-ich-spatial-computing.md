## Abstract

Spatial computing creates new possibilities for documenting, interpreting, and presenting intangible cultural heritage. Starting from the development of spatial computing and the changing requirements of cultural digitization, this paper examines the role and design value of the digital museum.

The article discusses how traditional cultural knowledge can be connected with contemporary digital technologies through spatially situated interaction and immersive presentation. It proposes design directions for digital museums that support cultural communication while opening new pathways for heritage preservation and public engagement.

**Keywords:** spatial computing; intangible cultural heritage; digitization; digital museums; digital design
