<section id="research" class="section" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">01 / Research</p><h2 id="research-title">Inclusive interaction. Meaningful experiences.</h2></div>
  <p class="section-intro">My research asks how handheld augmented reality can become more usable and accessible for older adults, particularly in cultural heritage settings.</p>
  <div class="research-grid">
    <article><span class="topic-number" aria-hidden="true">01</span><h3>Human–computer interaction</h3><p>Understanding how people interact with digital systems, with attention to usability, agency, and everyday experience.</p></article>
    <article><span class="topic-number" aria-hidden="true">02</span><h3>Handheld AR &amp; accessibility</h3><p>Exploring readable interfaces, approachable interactions, and comfortable mobile AR experiences for older adults.</p></article>
    <article><span class="topic-number" aria-hidden="true">03</span><h3>Digital cultural heritage</h3><p>Connecting people with cultural stories and knowledge through immersive interpretation and digital preservation.</p></article>
